## TIER 3: Human-Only

Things that genuinely require you — because of account creation, login
credentials, terms acceptance, payment, CAPTCHA, or tools I can't run.

| # | Asset | Why it's human-only |
|---|-------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Actual video screen recordings** (smooth scrolling code, terminal output as video not screenshots) | Requires launching OBS or a screen recorder, configuring capture region, starting/stopping recording. I can't launch arbitrary desktop apps. *Workaround: I take still screenshots and we use Ken Burns / pan-and-scan effects in the video editor to simulate scrolling.* |
| 2 | **Stock footage downloads from gated sites** | Pexels and Pixabay are browsable without login, but some video downloads require account creation or CAPTCHA. *Workaround: I find the exact URLs and you click download, or we use AI-generated B-roll stand-ins.* |
| 3 | **Royalty-free music** | YouTube Audio Library requires Google login. Freesound.org requires account. Epidemic Sound, Artlist, etc. require paid subscriptions. *Workaround: I identify specific tracks and you download them, or you use a tool like Suno/Udio to generate 90s-style background music from a prompt I write.* |
| 4 | **Sound effects** (card shuffles, card slaps, Win95 startup) | Same gating as music — freesound.org needs an account. The Win95 startup sound exists everywhere but downloading it involves clicking through pages. *Workaround: I find the exact URLs.* |
| 5 | **Final video assembly** | Compositing all assets into a timeline, syncing narration audio, adding transitions — requires a video editor (DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, CapCut, etc.) that I can't operate. *This is the true human-only step, unless we use a programmatic video tool like Remotion, ffmpeg scripting, or an AI video API.* |
| 6 | **TTS narration generation** (if using ElevenLabs, PlayHT, etc.) | Most high-quality TTS services require account + API key. *Workaround: you paste the script, or I call an API if you provide the key as an env variable.* |
| 7 | **YouTube upload & metadata** | Requires your Google/YouTube login. Obviously human-only. |

**Tier 3 total: 7 items. And most have workarounds that reduce the human part
to a single click or credential entry.**

## Recommended Execution Order

1. **Tier 1 first** — I generate all diagrams, slides, mockups, and code cards.
   This is the biggest batch and has zero dependencies. Pure output.

2. **Tier 2 second** — I take terminal screenshots and source historical images
   from the web. Some of this can happen in parallel with Tier 1.

3. **Tier 3 last** — You handle the handful of gated steps: download music/SFX
   from your accounts, generate TTS narration, do screen recordings if we want
   actual video instead of still-to-pan, assemble in your editor, upload.

Each tier's output feeds the next. By the time you sit down for Tier 3, all the
raw materials are already collected and waiting.
