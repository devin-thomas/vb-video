2. 1–5 s: title holds without animation.

4. 6–7 s: close into a clean outgoing frame.

Use 1920×1080 with the shared safe area and readable system fonts. Motion uses 30 fps and deterministic `renderAt(seconds)`; timing here is a proposed local edit, not final narration sync.
