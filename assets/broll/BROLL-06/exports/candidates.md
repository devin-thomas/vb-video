# BROLL-06 — Pentium-era PC booting: candidate report

**Status:** produced. Candidates were motion reviewed in-page; the rank-1 clip was then **acquired** on 2026-09-15 under Devin's approval and verified on the real file. Release blocked (R14 open) — acquisition is not clearance.
**Accessed:** 2026-09-15.

> **Label that binds every use of the acquired clip:** it shows a beige CRT with a blank screen in a dim room. It is **not a boot and not a power-on**, and **no CPU, model or date identifies a Pentium**. It is a **period-setting cutaway only**. Never label it a boot; never caption or imply "Pentium".
**Shot wanted:** a period desktop PC powering on, with a plausible CRT and OS (BIOS POST, memory count, Windows 95/3.x startup). Horizontal, with an 8–12 s usable segment.

## Bottom line
- **No free clip shows a Pentium-era PC booting or a period power-on.** This is based on viewed frames, not page text.
  - The best free shot is a period CRT PC in a room, with no boot.
  - The only free footage of a real boot process is a low-resolution POST-card close-up.
  - The one free "power-on" clip is a modern LCD monitor, so it is rejected.
- **The leads that do show a period boot are YouTube videos and are rights-blocked (H02).** These are LGR's AST Advantage 622, whose creator describes it as a 100 MHz Pentium running Windows 95, and "Old computer boot up Windows 95".
- **How frames were viewed.** During scouting, clips were played in-page in the in-app browser and screenshots were taken at preview size. For the rank-1 clip this was **superseded on 2026-09-15**: the file was acquired and frames were extracted and inspected at native 4096×2160 and again at 720p. The other candidates were never inspected above preview resolution.

## Review questions
*All of these remain open after acquisition. Downloading the clip answered none of them.*

1. **Fallback when no free clip shows a boot.** Nothing free shows a period boot — now confirmed against the acquired full-resolution file, not just previews — so which should we do?
   - (a) Use the Pexels period set shot (optionally cut with the POST-code insert) and let narration carry "booting".
   - (b) Pursue creator permission for the LGR or Jaime Cobo Vicente footage via H02.
   - (c) Commission a separately approved, labeled stand-in (self-shot on real hardware, or AI-generated). This needs a new assignment.
2. **Third-party on-screen UI.** Windows 95/3.x splash screens are Microsoft-copyrighted UI, and BIOS vendor logos are third-party marks. Is incidental on-screen OS/BIOS UI acceptable if option (b) or (c) is chosen?
3. **Pentium claim.** Neither free candidate identifies a CPU, and the acquired 4K file shows no PC case at all. The edit must not caption or imply "Pentium" over them, and must not label the acquired clip a boot. Is that acceptable?
4. **Free Art License.** Is its attribution and Article 4 (larger-work) condition acceptable for the final video, if the Commons clip is used?
5. **Resolution.** Is a roughly 2.6× upscale of a 640×416 clip visually acceptable for a brief insert?
6. **Visible monitor brand (new after acquisition).** The acquired clip's bezel reads "ViewSonic E70" at full resolution, though not at 720p. Is an identifiable monitor brand acceptable under the Pexels term against implying endorsement by brands shown, and should the shot be framed or scaled so the wordmark is not prominent?
