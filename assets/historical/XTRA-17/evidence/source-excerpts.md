## Independent corroboration outside the site

Jay Roxe (Microsoft), "An Open Letter to the Community", MSDN blog, 17 March 2005. It is archived by Microsoft at `https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/archive/blogs/jroxe/an-open-letter-to-the-community` (accessed 2026-09-15). The author says he mailed each Microsoft MVP who had signed the petition at classicvb.org/petition, and he gives Microsoft's reasons for its managed-code direction. This confirms from Microsoft's side that the petition existed and that MVPs signed it. Signature figures quoted by commenters under that post were not used.
