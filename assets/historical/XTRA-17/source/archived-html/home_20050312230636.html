Support Classic VB...

Sign the Petition

A request has gone out, originating with the Microsoft MVPs, for continuation of support of Classic Visual Basic. The suggested method is including a version of VB.COM within the Visual Studio product line. If your future includes supporting a Classic VB codebase, you really owe it to yourself to ask whether your support of this request could make your path forward more predictable. Currently, mainstream support for VB6 ends on March 31, 2005.
