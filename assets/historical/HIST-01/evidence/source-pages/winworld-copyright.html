For acceptance into our software library, a software title must meet our strict criteria for abandonware or pre-release software. For us to consider a software title 'abandonware', it must have an initial release date no later than January 1, 2001 and no longer being sold or supported by the original publisher or a licensed distributor. Pre-release software (alpha and beta releases) are subject to a more flexible criteria and ultimately the discretion of the WinWorld team.
