## Tests actually run

1. **Source identity.** SHA-256 of the served access.lst (2026-09-15) and of the Wayback raw capture `20100209024249id_` are both `883c34aefc363c4acaa5b1db9586e446b69fb9961935b70a666799ce6b875bc2`. `source/original.txt` has the same hash and 153281 bytes. The build script checks the hash on every run.
2. **Listing text fidelity (automated, in build_frame.py).** The SVG was parsed back with ElementTree. All 22 `class="listing"` text nodes equal the CP437-decoded, CRLF-split source lines, including trailing spaces. Output: `verified 22 listing lines against source/original.txt (CP437, CRLF split)`.
3. **Glyph coverage.** Every character in the decoded file, including the block and box-drawing characters and U+00A0, exists in Consolas's cmap. No fallback font is needed.
4. **Layout checks (automated, font advance widths).** The listing panel spans x=120–1425.6, with text ending at 1409.6. The sidebar starts at x=1465.6 and is 334.4 px wide, ending at 1800. Last baselines: listing 888, sidebar 880; both are ≤ 1008. The first run exited with "line numbers overflow safe area"; the columns were moved right by 12 px and the build re-run.
5. **Export size.** PIL reports (1920, 1080) RGB. The 720p proof is 1280×720, resized with Lanczos.
6. **Delivery validator.** See the result below.

## Manual visual inspection

Viewed by the worker on 2026-09-15:

- **Full size.** The whole 1920×1080 frame, plus four native-resolution 1:1 crops that together cover it: (100,60)–(1000,560), (900,60)–(1820,560), (100,540)–(1000,1040) and (900,540)–(1820,1040). The crops were kept in the session scratchpad, not the package.
- **720p.** `qa/editorial-frame-720p.png` at 1280×720.

Findings:
- The listing reads correctly against `evidence/source-excerpts.md`. Size and date columns stay aligned in Consolas, and nothing is clipped or wrapped.
- The line 541 header shows the CP437 block characters (█▓▒░) as Consolas glyphs. The ▒ and ░ shades render as fine dither patterns and look slightly busy at 720p. These are the file's characters in a modern monospace font, not a pixel reproduction of a DOS screen font. The label says it is a typeset rendering.
- The gap marker "source lines 549–585 not shown" sits on a panel-colored plate, so the dashed rule does not cross it.
- The gold outline encloses lines 591–592 and clears the text.
- **First render, rejected.** The `=` rules overran the panel border, the dashed rule crossed the gap label, and the sidebar title passed x=1800. The cause was PIL `getlength` measuring narrower than cairo draws. Widths now come from fontTools advance widths, and the second render fixed all three faults.
- At 720p the listing text is about 19 px and legible. The line numbers are about 13 px: small, but they are only a display layer. The sidebar text is about 17 px and legible.
- The frame contains no private information, invented UI, fake terminal chrome or watermark. The only person named anywhere in the file (line 673) is outside the excerpt.

## Content and editorial checks

- **Subject and era.** A real board file catalog with its board name (THE ACCESS SYSTEM - HUNTSVILLE, AL) and date (09/09/1992) printed in the file itself. That is early 1990s. It was generated by WGM-DMS v.3.5.
- **Script-supplement status.** This fills the "maybe a BBS file listing" visual at SCRIPT.md:595–597. It is not an ASSET_PLAN.md row.
- **Runtime claims.** Checked in `evidence/claim-checks.json`:
  - The runtime shipping separately is supported.
  - The VBRUN300.DLL name is supported with a caveat: the listing shows VBRUN.ZIP, which is pre-VB3, and VB4's runtimes are VB40016/VB40032.DLL.
  - The 1.5 MB figure and "nearly ten minutes at 14.4k" are **not supported as stated**. These go to the Writing Lead as a review question; War/SCRIPT.md was not edited.
- **Rights.** Unresolved (`evidence/rights.json`). Release stays blocked.
