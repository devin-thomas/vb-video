## Date evidence for the narration claim

Dartmouth College ITS history timeline, 1960s (<https://www.dartmouth.edu/its-tools/archive/history/timeline/1960s.html>, accessed 2026-09-15): records that on 1 May 1964 at 4:00 a.m. DTSS ran simultaneous programs, credited to Kemeny, Kurtz and students, with the program `10 PRINT 2+2 / 20 END`. It also records that the Kiewit Computation Center was dedicated 2 December 1966.

Dartmouth News, Bill Platt, 15 April 2014, "Back to BASIC: Computing's Future Was Born at Dartmouth" (<https://home.dartmouth.edu/news/2014/04/back-basic-computings-future-was-born-dartmouth>): states that in 1964 Kemeny and Kurtz, with a handful of undergraduates, introduced time-sharing and BASIC.
